# 09 — The Castle That Fell Through Its Toilet

## Narration (plain, 190 words)
He climbed up a castle toilet. From the bottom. This was Château Gaillard, the toughest castle in France. Richard the Lionheart built it on a cliff in just two years. Three rings of walls, a moat, towers everywhere. The French king swore he'd take it even with walls of iron. Richard just laughed. He'd hold it even with walls of butter. So in twelve oh three, the French came. They dug, they smashed, they starved the place for months. The first wall fell. The second wouldn't budge. Then, the story goes, a soldier named Bogis looked up and spotted a little stone box sticking out of the wall. With a hole in the bottom. Yep. That's the toilet. He and a few friends crawled up the chute, popped out in the chapel, and threw open the gate. The defenders panicked, and soon the whole castle surrendered. You see, castle toilets stuck out of the walls on purpose. Everything just dropped outside. Clean, simple, genius. Except a hole that lets things out also lets things in. The castle that could hold with walls of butter was taken through the toilet.
